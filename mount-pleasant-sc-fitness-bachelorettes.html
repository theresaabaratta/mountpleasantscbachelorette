Bachelorette Fitness Classes in Mount Pleasant SC

The best fitness classes in Mount Pleasant SC for a bachelorette group are the ones nobody takes too seriously — pole dancing, bungee, sunrise yoga and sexy dance classes that end in laughter and great photos.

Why Add a Class to the Weekend?

A private group class breaks the ice between friends who've never met, gives the bride a moment in the spotlight, and balances out a weekend of brunch and Shem Creek cocktails. Women's fitness studios in Mount Pleasant regularly host private bachelorette parties — book 3–6 weeks out for Saturday slots and confirm your headcount, since most studios price per person with a group minimum.

Pole Dancing

$$ · $35–$65 per person

Inversions Pole Fitness on S Shelmore Blvd runs private, beginner-friendly bachelorette pole parties — no experience needed. Instructors teach a short routine, spins and poses, and the studio's flower wall is made for the group photo. They also offer Polga (pole + yoga) and conditioning if anyone gets hooked.

Sexy Dance Classes: Chair, Burlesque & Twerk

$$ · $35–$60 per person

Not everyone wants to climb a pole. A chair-dance, burlesque or twerk class keeps it flirty and low-pressure — heels optional, confidence guaranteed. Inversions offers these styles for private parties, and it's the move that gets pulled out on the dance floor later that night.

How to Fit a Class Into Your Weekend

Friday night: A pole or sexy dance class is the perfect kickoff — everyone's energy is high and it sets the tone.

Saturday morning: Bungee before the beach burns off Friday night and gets you IOP-ready.

Sunday: Yoga or Pilates, then brunch. Pair it with the full plan on our Mount Pleasant bachelorette weekend itinerary.
